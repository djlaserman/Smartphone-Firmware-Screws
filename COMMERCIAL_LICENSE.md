# Commercial License — Request & Terms (Template / Informational)

**IMPORTANT:** This document is an informational summary of the commercial licensing process for this repository. The formal, legally binding commercial license will be a separate written agreement signed by both parties.

## What Qualifies as Commercial Use

Commercial Use includes, but is not limited to:

- Use by for-profit entities in production or internal systems
- Distribution or sale of software that includes or is derived from this Software
- Use to provide paid services (SaaS, hosting, support, consulting where the Software is used to generate revenue)
- Embedding the Software in a product that is sold or licensed for a fee

## License Grant (upon agreement)

Upon acceptance of your request and execution of a written license agreement, the copyright holder will grant a commercial license describing the granted rights (for example: redistribution, sublicensing, number of installations, territory, duration).

## Fees and Payment

Commercial licensing requires:

- A one-time license fee (paid up-front)
- A recurring subscription/license fee (paid annually or on a schedule agreed in the commercial license)

Fee amounts are determined case-by-case based on the scope of use. The copyright holder will provide a quote after reviewing your request. Fees must be paid in full prior to the commercial license taking effect.

## Term and Renewal

The commercial license term, renewal conditions, and termination rights will be specified in the written license agreement.

## Support and Maintenance

Support, maintenance, and custom development (if any) will be handled according to the commercial license and any separate service agreements.
